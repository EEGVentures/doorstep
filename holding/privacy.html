Privacy notice

For the first-access sign-up. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Who we are

Doorstep is a UK app for gigs, comedy and theatre near you. For this sign-up, Doorstep is the data controller. You can reach us about your data at privacy@doorsteptickets.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Whether you ticked the box for occasional emails, with the exact wording you agreed to and when.
  • Where you came from, if the link you used said (for example a social post).
  • A scrambled, one-way code made from your internet address, used only to stop automated sign-ups. We don’t store the address itself, and these codes are deleted after a day.

What we use it for

  • To tell you when Doorstep opens. You asked for this by signing up, so we’ll email you when accounts open in February 2027.
  • Occasional emails about good nights out near you, only if you ticked the box. This is based on your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

We never sell your details or share them with promoters, venues or partners.

Who handles it for us

Your sign-up is stored with Supabase (database, hosted in London) and the page runs on Vercel. When we email you, we use Resend. Each works under a contract that lets them use your data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

Until Doorstep opens and we’ve sent your first-access email. After that we keep it only if you ticked the box for occasional emails, until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If you create a Doorstep account, your account’s own privacy policy takes over.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or to stop emails, at any time. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or write to privacy@doorsteptickets.com. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
